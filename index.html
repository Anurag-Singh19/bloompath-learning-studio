ABOUT YOUR DATA

Private by default

Your email and learning progress are saved in the project’s Supabase database. Each account can read and change only its own progress. The site owner’s allowlisted admin account can view account emails and saved progress, reminders, and resume details to manage the service.

Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth and are never visible to the site owner. Do not put sensitive personal information into your learning notes or resume.

Account sync needs the project’s Supabase URL, publishable key, and database migration. Guest progress works now in this browser.
